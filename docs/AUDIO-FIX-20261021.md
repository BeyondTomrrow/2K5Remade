# In-game audio: pitch warble, crowd, SOFTDRINK skip (2026-10-21/22)

User report (2026-10-21): "play list doesnt work when i skip a song in softdrink plays a song cant
skip no crowd audio in both and its glitched sounds like deep voice filter and then alvin and the
chipmunks".

Evidence sheet: `logs\audio\audio-fix-sheet.png`. Clips (21 s each, the same moment -- SOFTDRINK
Main Menu, R3 pressed): `logs\audio\before-clip.wav` (two presses, the song plays on) and
`logs\audio\after-clip.wav` (one press, the next song starts).

## Results

| # | Complaint | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "plays a song, can't skip" (SOFTDRINK) | R3 queued a skip that nothing took: the retail music frame routine 0x00280620 is reached only by the tail jump at the end of `sub_000F6420`, which SOFTDRINK changed, so the interpreter ran 0x00280620 itself and the generated code's `LOCALMUSIC_RETAIL_NEXT` hook never ran. | **Fixed** (commit 6baaef8), verified |
| 2 | "deep voice ... then alvin and the chipmunks" | Every voice's pitch measured once a second and at every write, in base and SOFTDRINK. No voice drifts while it plays except the game's own crowd-reaction stream, whose frequencies are **the same as in xemu** (title crowd swell: 19644 -> 23352 Hz in both). Commentary and music are fixed-pitch; 3D sounds move p99 29 cents (doppler) and play exactly as long as their sample rate says. The +/-2000-cent "bursts" a per-second view shows at every snap are new sounds on reused voices. No emulator-side pitch defect was found; nothing changed. | Measured; not reproduced as an emulation defect |
| 3 | "no crowd audio in both" | Retail: the crowd beds (0x4A-0x4F) never go silent in a match (gain 0.10-0.31) and the reaction layer (0x44/0x45) is on top. SOFTDRINK: its own crowd code lets the bed decay to silence between reactions (silent 35 % of match seconds) and does not use the reaction layer; **its native (non-interpreted) v0.6 build does the same**, so it is the pack's crowd logic, not the interpreter or the APU. | Measured; SOFTDRINK behaviour documented, retail OK |

### 1. SOFTDRINK skip

Before (run `s1`, SOFTDRINK v0.6.1, the build before the fix): three R3 presses in the Main Menu,
`[MUSIC] R3: next cribmusic track requested (now 0)` three times, never an advance; the song keeps
playing (`logs\audio\s1-skip-spec.png`, presses at 295.5 s and 307.5 s of `s1.wav`).

Cause: `nfl2k5_retail_music_take_next()` is consumed by the gen patch `LOCALMUSIC_RETAIL_NEXT` at
the top of `sub_00280620`. The only way into 0x00280620 is `jmp 0x280620` at 0x000F6505, the end of
`sub_000F6420`; SOFTDRINK's XBE differs inside `sub_000F6420`, so it runs interpreted and the
interpreter follows the jump into the retail routine's bytes -- the native C (and its hook) never
runs. SOFTDRINK also replaced the stop (0x0027FEC0 -> `jmp 0x014DFC41`) and the advance
(0x0027F040 -> `jmp 0x014DFE72`); the advance with the stop before it clears its "same song again"
flag and asks its shuffle for the next song (state 2), which is what a skip should do.

Fix (`src/nfl2k5_local_music.cpp`): an interpreter hook at 0x00280620 takes the request and returns
into the stop with the advance and 0x00280620 as return addresses (the pack's own versions run);
the second arrival restores eax/ecx/edx (as the native hook does) and lets the routine run. The
log now shows every press (`R3: next ... requested`, `R3: skip the playing song ...`,
`R3: nothing to skip ...`) and every advance.

After (run `s2`, same pack, same sandbox): three presses at 247.3 / 281.3 / 301.3 s of `s2.wav`,
three `[MUSIC] R3: the retail player advances (stop + next on the guest thread)`, a different song
after each (`logs\audio\s2-skip-spec.png`, short silences of 50-320 ms where the stream restarts).

The playlist-wins work (the player's own Jukebox playlist over SOFTDRINK's shuffle) is the music
agent's (docs/MUSIC-FIX-20261021.md); a playlist song is skipped by the existing `end_pos` path,
which is native code in both builds.

### 2. Pitch

Runs: `b1`/`b3` retail (render scale 9-12 as in the player's settings), `b2` retail at scale 1,
`s1`/`s2` SOFTDRINK v0.6.1; 5-8 minutes of CPU-vs-CPU play each (A every 6 s for the coin toss and
the play calls). `logs\audio\pitch-before.png` = the per-second view of `b1`;
`logs\audio\pitch-after.png` = per-sound drift (`b3`) + xemu comparison.

* **Who writes pitch.** `[PITCHSET]` stacks: a 3D sound's frequency is set by the game's sound
  state machine `sub_0003DBC0` (0x3DE85 -> DirectSound SetFrequency 0x004478C5) right before
  `VOICE_ON`; while it plays, DirectSound's 3D update (game calls at 0x3E19C position /
  0x3E1C5 velocity, 0x447A4B / 0x447A80) rewrites it by 1-60 units (0.3-18 cents): doppler.
  Per sound (1295 3D sounds in `b3`): median 0 c, p95 11 c, p99 29 c. The 8 larger ones are voices
  the 3D engine re-binds to a new sound without a new `VOICE_ON` (CBO jumps back).
* **Play duration.** 1361 3D one-shots in `b0`: time from `VOICE_ON` to the title's `VOICE_OFF`
  over (LBO / frequency): median 1.04 (the excess is the title's stop latency). A sound decoded at
  the wrong rate would be 0.5 or 2.
* **Commentary** (voices 0x46-0x49, centre bin) and **music** (0x44/0x45 in the menus, the
  playlist on 0x84 at 44.1 kHz): one pitch per clip, never moving (in-second spread 0).
* **The one voice that does slide** is the crowd-reaction stream (track 0, voices 0x44/0x45 in a
  match): the game sets its pitch in cents (`sub_0003D0D0`, object field +0x2150 x 1/1200 octave,
  `sub_000CF830`) between -200 c (calm, deep) and about +150 c (excited) together with its volume.
  xemu oracle (`tools\audio\xemu_freq.gdb`, a hardware breakpoint on the same SetFrequency call,
  `logs\audio\xemu-freq.log`): the title-screen crowd swell is 19644, 19941, ... 23352 Hz peak, then
  the slow decay; the port: 19642 ... 23355 Hz. Same values (the step count differs only because
  xemu ran slowed by the breakpoint); the other streams match too (12000, 16000, 22050, 48000 Hz).
  This is the original game's crowd, and the most likely thing heard as "deep, then chipmunk"
  when the crowd bed under it is quiet.
* **Pacing.** VP frames per second median 1500 (nominal 1500) in every run; voice-lock waits 0.1-
  2.3 s and ISR-stall waits 1-5 s over 10 minutes; no dropped buffers; 30-90 XAudio2 underruns per
  run, a third of them during loads. A first baseline (`b0`, with `RECOMP_APU_VOICE_LOG` on) ran
  2-4 % slow with 6-23 underruns per 10 s in play -- caused by the logging itself (fprintf under
  the APU lock); without it (`b1`..`s2`) play runs at full rate.

### 3. Crowd

`tools\audio\crowd_report.py` (gains from the `[PITCH]` lines = volume x envelope as heard):

| Run | Crowd beds 0x4A-0x4F | Silent | Reaction 0x44/45 | Stereo side |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| b3 retail | mean 0.312 | 0 % of match seconds | mean 0.214 | -36.4 dBFS |
| s2 SOFTDRINK v0.6.1 | mean 0.232 | 35 % of match seconds | not used | -41.0 dBFS |
| n1 SOFTDRINK v0.6 **native** build (`mods\packs\SOFTDRINK_2K28_v0.6-v0.6\native`) | 0.10-0.17, decaying | 10 of 29 5-s samples | - | - |
| b0 retail (5-s samples) | 0.10 | 0 of 101 | - | - |

SOFTDRINK ships its own 22 kHz crowd (voices 0x4A/0x4B at pitch -4597; retail's are 12 kHz) and
drives it like a reaction: up to ~0.25 at an event, then down to 0 over ~10-15 s, silent until the
next one (`s2.stderr.log`). The natively recompiled v0.6 pack does the same, so this is not the
interpreter. Whether it is also silent on an Xbox was not checked (xemu needs a focused window and
controller input to get into a match).

No host-side control muted anything: `[MIXCTL]` (every change of the bed gain, commentary gain or
music mute) logged nothing in any match, `bed=1.00 comm=1.00 mute_music=0` every second.

The player's sliders (Options > Settings, floats at 0xE601B4, `[PRES] sliders`): TV Broadcast 0.65,
PA 0.50, Sound Effects 0.50, Crowd 0.50, Player Voice 0.50, Menu Music 0.50, and a seventh 0.00.
Run `b4` raised the seventh to 1.00 (`NFL2K5_AUDIO_SLIDERS`, the save untouched): no group moved
(mean match gains b3 -> b4: crowd beds 0.050 -> 0.051, reaction layer 0.100 -> 0.110,
commentary 0.235 -> 0.259, 3D sounds 0.253 -> 0.290 -- different plays, same levels), so it is not
a hidden crowd mute. The crowd slider itself is at half.

## Regressions checked

* Menu music: femusic on 0x44/0x45 at -4597 constant; the Crib/Menus playlist (WMA, voice 0x84)
  constant at 44.1 kHz; SOFTDRINK's shuffle plays and now skips.
* Commentary: one pitch per clip, routed to the centre bin, `comm=1.00`.
* Draft show (M12): not re-run. This work's runtime changes are (a) logging that is off unless
  `RECOMP_APU_PITCH_LOG` / `RECOMP_APU_PITCH_STACK` is set, plus `[MIXCTL]` lines on a host mix
  change, and (b) the interpreter hook at 0x00280620 (interpreted packs only). The draft show's bed
  ducking goes through `xbox_AudioSetGameGain` / `xbox_AudioMuteGameMusic`, unchanged apart from the
  log line.

## Instrumentation and tools

Background mode only (`RECOMP_BACKGROUND=1`, `RECOMP_AUDIO_MUTE=1`), its own exe copy
`build\Release\NFL2K5_audio.exe`, `NFL2K5_NO_HOST_PAD=always`, its own press file,
`NFL2K5_PRIVATE_PACKS=1`, a sandbox root `E:\eras-sbx\audio-root` (junctions + a sparse copy of
`saves\`, its own `mods\active-pack.ini`), a user folder copied from the player's own settings
(presentation NBC SNF, render scale). Harness `tools\audio\audio_test.ps1` (`launch [-SoftDrink]
[-SourceExe <exe>]`, `quickgame`, `play`, `press`, `shot`, `stop`, `clean`).

Vendored APU (`external\xboxrecomp\src\apu`, gitignored; this work's hunks only:
`docs\AUDIO-FIX-20261021-xboxrecomp.diff`):

* `RECOMP_APU_PITCH_LOG=1`: once a second `[PITCH] qpc= t= vpframes= queue=min..max submits=
  dropped= underruns= bed= comm= mute_music= wav= lockwaits= lockwait_ms= stalls= stall_ms=`
  (`wav=` = frames in the RECOMP_AUDIO_WAV recording, so log and capture line up exactly), then per
  voice that played: pitch range, envelope pitch scale, filter envelope, rate range, title pitch
  writes, `gain=avg..max` as heard (after the mutes), `C` for the commentary bin.
* `RECOMP_APU_PITCH_STACK=1|3d`: every pitch change with the guest stack, every voice start.
* `[MIXCTL]`: each change of the host mix controls.

Analysis: `tools\audio\pitch_report.py` (figure, per-voice table, 3D swing per second),
`pitch_within_sound.py` (per-sound drift, xemu overlay), `pitch_jumps.py` (pitch changes vs frame
time and their callers), `crowd_report.py`, `voice_dump.py` (RECOMP_APU_VOICE_DUMP -> WAVs),
`xemu_freq.gdb` (xemu: every SetFrequency of the sound state machine).

xemu was run minimized with `[audio] volume_limit = 0` and the retail ISO for the comparison; the
player's `xemu.toml` was restored afterwards (backup `xemu.toml.audiofix-backup`).
