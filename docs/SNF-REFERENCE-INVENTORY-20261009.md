## Version distinction

The requested Brady / Mahomes compact black-glass insert and circular clock pod belong to the 2022 system. The research also found a 2026 refresh and a different 2026 wraparound package. These are related NBC families, not interchangeable designs. Preserve the user's supplied 2022 reference as the target for the current package; do not silently combine a 2026 scorebar with its old inserts.

## Implementation implications

- User's Brady reference is a five-column QB card: COMP, ATT, YDS, TD/INT, RATING. It has a dark translucent surface tinted by team color, portrait overlapping left, position / jersey-number tag, name / context rail and curved chrome end. Other player roles need their own meaningful columns within that geometry.
- Keep the independent player card, scorebug-attached THIS DRIVE insert, scoring-drive foldout, large feature, comparison and team table as separate event families.
- Verified 2023 and 2025 live captures use an inline play clock beside the quarter, while the designer GIF uses a hanging tab. These are documented broadcast variants; the live-game implementation should choose its intended variant consistently.
- Keep timing values documented as implementation estimates until they are measured from viewable source footage. Browser GIF screenshots verified forms and phases, not frame-accurate durations.
- A generic horizontal slide alone does not reproduce the observed component reveals. Logo / portrait, plate, rail, table and curve have distinct entry phases.
- No exact 2022 live replay wipe was verified here. The proposed chrome-arc / vapor-light replay transition is an inferred recreation pending a directly watched replay transition. It must not be described as an exact broadcast copy.
- The creator's 2026 text explicitly describes team-logo-to-portrait wipes and city-tricode exits, but that does not prove those were the 2022 replay wipe.
