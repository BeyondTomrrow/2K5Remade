## 1. Drop-in mod packs, widescreen, player face fix
*Commit 300549b, 02:50*

- **x86 interpreter for mod packs.** Mods such as SOFTDRINK 2K28 change the
  game's own code. The native build was converted from the retail executable,
  so a changed function would run the old code. At boot, every converted
  function whose bytes differ in the pack's executable is now marked, and calls
  to it go to a built-in x86 interpreter that runs the pack's real bytes. The
  rest stays native. SOFTDRINK 2K28 v0.4 and v0.5 run this way at 60 fps
  (about 1,100 of 33,500 functions interpreted).
- **mods\patches drop folder.** Dropping a `.2k5patch` or a disc image there
  installs it in the background. Packs without native code run on the main
  build.
- **Widescreen** is built into Video Settings; the old Widescreen pack was
  retired.
- **Player face and body holes fixed.** Some models drew with gaps, mostly in
  faces. Triangle strips were not being split where the game switches drawing
  methods (DRAW_ARRAYS / ARRAY_ELEMENT) in the middle of a model.

## 2. Expanded limits, per-user settings, benchmark tooling
*Commit 915cb6c, 04:34*

- **Create a Team stadium list: 67 to 82.** The game hides 15 of its stadium
  records from the picker. The list is copied into guest memory with the 15
  added, its three readers and seven bounds are patched after a byte check, and
  the patched functions run in the interpreter. Verified in game: after Aloha
  Stadium come Practice Facility, Visual Concepts Dome, the Super Bowl
  stadiums, ESPN Stadium, Cheesesteak Dome and the rest.
- **Franchise length: 30 to 95 seasons** (through 2099, where the game's own
  dates stop working). The instruction is patched and runs in the interpreter;
  a 31st season has not been played yet.
- Both are on by default (`expanded_limits` in `nfl2k5_game.ini`).
- **Settings, caches and logs in the user profile**
  (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\NFL2K5-PC`), so the game runs without administrator rights
  under Program Files. Saves and mods stay in the game folder. A portable
  layout is kept for the development checkout, `portable.txt`, or
  `NFL2K5_PORTABLE=1`.
- **Tools:** `tools\bench.ps1` and `bench-report.py` (benchmarks),
  `tools\audit-xbox-limits.py` (byte-exact checks of known limits),
  `tools\drive-menu.ps1` (scripted menu runs).

*Commit 5f7b3ce, 06:14*

- **Up to four controllers** on the game's four ports for local multiplayer:
  Xbox controllers through XInput; DualShock 4, DualSense and DualSense Edge
  over USB and Bluetooth through HID.
- Hot-plugging reported to the game as insert/remove, radial stick and trigger

  saved and reset, two backups created.

## 5. Controller Setup in the game's own menu
*Commit 26b44a5, 11:53*

- Options > Controller Setup shows a picture of the controller in use (Xbox
  Series, DualShock 4 or DualSense, from photographs) with each action's label
  beside its button, in the game's own screen style.
- **Custom Control layout:** any action can be moved to another button. The
  button being changed is highlighted on the picture and the screen waits for
  the new press ("Press Y or any button for Juke Left"). Offense and defense
  have separate layouts; defaults can be restored.
- Button prompts use Kenney's free Input Prompts icons (CC0, licence shipped in
  `docs\third-party`).

## 6. Texture packs
*Commit 97dba95, 12:21*

- A PNG in `mods\textures`, named after the texture it replaces, is used in its
  place at whatever resolution it was made, with mipmaps generated.
- A dump mode writes out the textures the game uses (decoded from their Xbox
  formats), so pack makers know what to replace.

## 7. Every team in Team Select
*Commit 8c9c2bb, 13:30*

- The disc ships 75 historic teams (the '85 Bears, the '72 Dolphins and the
  rest) that retail only reaches through Create a Team > Load Historic Team.
- Quick Game, Practice and First Person Football now step past the last current
  team into the historic teams, then into any team in `mods\teams`, and wrap
  around in both directions. Stepping onto one loads it with the game's own
  loader into that side's spare team slot. A slot holding a user-created team is
  never touched, and online play keeps the retail list.

## 8. New teams with their own identity
*Commit 1babf7e, 15:18*

- A new team is a folder, `mods\teams\<name>`, never a repaint of an existing
  club. `team.json` holds its city, nickname, abbreviation, colours and a
  two-character asset code of its own (`Z1`, `Z2`, `Q1`...).
- Its uniform and crowd files live in `files\` and are packed at startup into
  one extra archive pack that the game reads like its own disc files.
- Any file the team doesn't have falls back to the base team it was built from
  (through a hook on the game's file-name checksum), so a team can start small.
- Verified: a new team in its own recoloured uniforms on the field, with its
  own name in the menus, prompts and scoreboard.

## 9. Team Creator
*Commits 1cae6a8 16:19, 7a0799c 16:30*

A desktop tool (`Team Creator.cmd`, Python with a simple window: Team, Uniforms,
Roster and Build tabs) that builds everything a new team needs straight into
`mods\teams`. It reads art and rosters from the player's own disc; no game files
are distributed with it.

- **Uniforms:** pick the club whose uniform cut, helmet shape and crowd the team
  starts from, then the team's colours. Every uniform and crowd texture is
  recoloured; whites, greys and blacks keep their shading.
- **Logos:** the logo goes on both helmet sides (one mirrored), the name on the
  bumper plate, and the logo and flip-chip textures are redrawn. Without a logo,
  one is drawn from the team's initials.
- **Roster import:** reads EA's roster database format. Madden NFL 21 (PC,
  little-endian) and NCAA Football 14 (PS3, big-endian) are verified. Pick a
  team in the file and its players come across: names, positions, numbers,
  height, weight and ratings mapped onto the game's 28 ratings.
- **Generated rosters** with jersey numbers inside each position's real range.
- **roster.csv** is applied by the game when the team loads: names, positions,
  numbers, body, skin, hand, ratings and depth chart order.

## 12. Team logos on the roster screens
*Commit 5b91b9d, 19:13*

- The roster card's logo chip and the backdrop logo come from two large picture
  files (`logos.cdf`, `flipchip.cdf`). Each is a set of fixed-size slots,
  indexed by a sorted list of name checksums and streamed in on demand.
- A new team's pictures now get slot numbers past the end of each list. When
  the game asks for one, it is read from `mods\teams\<name>\cache\` and handed
  back through the game's own read completion.
- The Team Creator writes those slots from the team's logo, outlined so it reads
  on any background, for every logo style the base team has.
- Verified: Alabama's script A on the roster card instead of the Chiefs'
  arrowhead.

## 13. Player photos

- `roster.csv` has a `photo` column. The Team Creator numbers a team's photos

## 15. Release packaging
*Commits 7a96060 20:44, b08b759 21:11*

- **Only listed teams ship.** `tools\build-release.ps1` stages `release\teams`:
  the 32 clubs' presentation files and each listed release team's own files.
  Files built from a disc (`files\`, `cache\`, `team.rost`) and unlisted test
  teams never ship. Before this, the installer would have copied every
  `team.json` and PNG under `mods\teams`.
- **The Claude team is built on install.** A built team is mostly recoloured
  game data, so the release ships Claude as a recipe (`creator.json`, roster,
  logos). `NFL2K5-TeamBuilder.exe` (the Team Creator's builder, packaged with
  PyInstaller, no Python needed) builds every recipe without a built roster from
  the player's own disc, right after setup links the disc. Recipes store paths
  relative to the team folder, so a team folder can be shared and rebuilt
  anywhere.
- Verified: the builder exe rebuilt Claude byte-for-byte identical on a clean
  layout; the installer compiles. A full install run has not been done yet.
- **Installer paths repaired:** four paths whose backslash escapes had turned
  into control characters (`assets\controls`, `docs\third-party`,
  `mods\textures`).

## 16. SOFTDRINK scorebug under HTML packages
*Commit 5249f87, 22:00*

- With an HTML broadcast package (such as NBC Sunday Night Football) on,
  SOFTDRINK 2K28's own scorebug stayed on screen under it.
- Cause: SOFTDRINK moves its bug by changing constants in two scorebug
  functions, so they run in the interpreter, and the native patches that hide
  the bug (and sample the quarterbacks for the HTML cards) never ran there.
- Fix: the interpreter accepts native hooks at guest instructions (optionally
  only when falling through from a given instruction, for a patch placed before
  a loop label) and calls the same C code at the same five places.
  `NFL2K5_INTERP_HOOKS=<hex mask>` selects them for diagnostics.
- Confirmed working in game.

## 17. Write-ups and research

- **Showcase page** of the project's work, and a public **day summary page**
  ("Game day notes") with screenshots.
- **Feasibility study: a connected NFL and college football universe.** Main
  findings: the original Franchise is built for exactly 32 teams (fixed season
  block, 22x17 schedule grid, 32-team front-office tables, fixed 720,044-byte
  save); added teams currently load into two spare slots and are not part of
  saves; the gameplay engine runs one match at a time. Recommended design: one
  PC-side universe database owning the calendar, both leagues and every player,
  with the game as the match engine and the native Franchise as an optional NFL
  client. Suggested order: permanent added teams, universe database and
  calendar, playing universe games with results captured, the NFL draft bridge,
  team switching, college systems, front-end presentation (SportsCenter, CFP
  show), highlight clips, eras.

## 18. Known issues and what is next

- **SOFTDRINK intermittent crash at startup** (`sub_003CAD50`, a divide by zero
  in what looks like stream/audio code). Seen with and without the scorebug
  hooks; not yet investigated.
- **SOFTDRINK has its own Team Select code**, so the historic and new teams
  don't appear in Team Select while it is active (left alone on purpose).
- **Install test:** a full run of the new installer, including the Claude team
  build, is still to do.
- **Teams:** permanent slots in the roster and saves (so new teams can join a
  franchise); a team's own Team Select card art; its abbreviation on the
  scorebug; real colleges for imported players (they show the template's);
  the default uniform style label; logos on pants and sleeves.
- **Team Creator:** more Madden and NCAA roster years once real files are
  tested; college and pro team types.
- **Limits:** players per team (65 stored, 53 active) and the created-team
  slots need save-format work; a 31st franchise season still needs playing.
