### D1 package

* **teams.csv** (`id,city,name,abbr,conference,division,template_entry,template_id,roster_file,community_slot,lineage_status`):
  28 franchises in the verified 1980 divisions. City, name and abbreviation come from
  `nfl_seasons_1970_1980.json`, and each is cross-checked against the 1980 identity in
  `franchise_events_1970_2025.json`. Teams are listed alphabetically within each division, not in
  the real final order, so an all-0-0-0 standings screen does not give away the 1980 finish.
  `roster_file` is relative to the package folder, with a forward slash (`rosters/colts.csv`).
* **Templates:** the PHASE2-PLAN 2.4 rule, implemented in code (`pick_template`). All 28 results
  equal the plan's table, and `--check` asserts it. DAL_1962 "texans" is excluded (its identity
  was never checked). For the Raiders, LA_1983 is nearer but rejected by the city rule.
* **Save slots:** each franchise's slot is found by its retail 2004 abbreviation and asserted
  unique. The unused slots are 16 JAX, 20 CAR, 24 BAL (Ravens) and 29 HOU (Texans), as the plan
  says.
* **Rosters** (`roster.csv` format of `nfl2k5_team_roster`: `first,last,pos,number,height,weight,years_pro,skin,face,hand,<28 ratings>,photo`):
  * Read from the `ros1980` payload through the mod tools' `RosterDocument`; its sha256
    `857ef4d4...c327` is asserted against the extraction.
  * Every row is joined to `data/eras/community_rosters/1980.json` by slot and list position, and
    the names must match. That is how the extractor's flags reach the sidecar.
  * Skin, face and hand are written as stored: the save record and the in-memory player use the
    same bit layout, `+0x18/+0x19/+0x20`, as the writer.
  * Photo is 39999 and no birth date is written.
  * Names are checked against the game-side reader: ASCII, no comma or quote, at most 31
    characters (the longest is 13).
* **Flags sidecars:** these match the plan's counts for the 28 used slots exactly: 24 duplicate
  players, 5 cloned bodies and 11 implausible bodies (8 heights of 60-61 in, 3 weights of
  404-405 lb). Heights and weights are kept as stored.
* **PROVENANCE.txt:**
  * the league sources, and the schedule sources and checks;
  * the roster label "COMMUNITY PS2 ROSTER (UNVERIFIED)" with the stem, payload, sha256 and parser;
  * the lineage per renamed slot, the template rule with every choice and its season gap;
  * the sha256 of every input.

## Findings the next milestones need

* **Row order vs. the stored depth fields.**
  * The plan's assumption that "list order = depth order" holds in 423 of 472 position groups.
    In those groups, the save's team list (grouped by position) follows the records' own
    rank/side depth fields, which for T, G, DE, DT, OLB, ILB and CB/WR are interleaved
    left/right chains.
  * In the other 49 groups (11 slots), one row's stored values would rank it above the rows
    listed before it, and the stored values are not a consistent chain. Example, SF QB: Montana
    rank 1, DeBerg 2, Barisich 0.
  * The package keeps list order, as the plan says. The writer will therefore rank such a row
    last, and each row is named in its flags file.
  * Which order is right in game is not known: T2.1 should look at one of these teams' depth
    screens (49ers, Bills, Giants, Raiders, Redskins, Broncos).
* **Ratings above 99.** 59 values (stamina 30, composure 11, leadership 11, consistency 5 (one
  of them 102), speed 1, aggressiveness 1) are written as 99. The game-side writer clamps to 99
  anyway, and each value is listed in the flags file.
* **schedule.csv has a sixth column, `historical`.** The plan's five fields come first, so a
  reader that wants only `game_id,week,date,away,home` can ignore it.
* **`game_id` format** is `1980w<week>-<away id>-<home id>`. It is safe as a file name and
  unique within the season.
* **The C reader** (`nfl2k5_team_roster`) splits on commas only. Every field in the package is
  free of commas and quotes, and all files are ASCII with `\n` line ends.

## Known defects and limits

* **Rosters are a community PS2 roster (unverified).** Nothing about players, ratings or bodies
  is checked against history. Defects stay visible in the flags sidecars:
  * 24 duplicates, 5 clones and 11 implausible bodies;
  * 49 depth-field disagreements;
  * 59 clamped ratings.
* **No ages.** The save's birth-year convention is "mixed or unclear", so no birth dates are
  written. In-game ages are meaningless, as the plan already notes.
* **Templates.** Their uniforms come from other seasons (gaps from -14 to +11 years). No fidelity
  is claimed, and `teams.csv` allows a per-franchise override.
* **Schedule sources.** PFR is the only per-game publisher. The second PFR capture and Wikipedia
  are not independent of it, and the NFL book confirms season totals only. PFR's kick-off times
  have no stated time zone and are kept out of `schedule.csv`. Overtime is not recorded.
* **Tool dependencies.**
  * The package builder needs `.tmp-2k-football-mod-tools/` (for `RosterDocument`).
  * A fresh cache needs network access for `--fetch` and `pdftotext` (mingw64); both
    were present here.
  * Wayback may serve a different capture than the one asked for. The capture actually
    served is the one recorded.
* **Version control.** `build/` and `logs/` are gitignored in this repository, so the four new
  scripts, the source caches and the logs are not tracked. `data/eras/` and `docs/eras/` are
  untracked as well. Nothing was committed.
