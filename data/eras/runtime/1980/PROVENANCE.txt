  and unverified: show them in game as "unverified community roster" ratings.

  Selection rule (M8, real players only; 45 real players at most, the 1980 NFL active roster
  limit, plus a last-resort filler where one is listed;
  the staging takes 42..53 rows, the game's minimum and the templates' player count): a traded
  player belongs to the team where he had more GS, then G; every player with a 1980 start plus
  the kicker with the most FGA and the punter with the most punts (always kept, so a team may
  pass 45 with starters); then position minimums (by games, real players only); then the most
  games played within position caps, up to 45; then players over a cap, up to 45. Minimums QB 2 K 1 P 1 HB 2 FB 1 WR 4 TE 2 T 3 G 3 C 1 DE 3 DT 2 OLB 2 ILB 2 CB 3 FS 1 SS 1;
  caps QB 3 K 2 P 2 HB 6 FB 4 WR 7 TE 5 T 6 G 6 C 3 DE 6 DT 6 OLB 6 ILB 6 CB 7 FS 4 SS 4. A minimum no real rated player can fill is a shortfall, listed per team, not
  filled. FILLERS only as a last resort: one per position in K 1 that no real rated player
  holds, and up to 42 rows for a team
  with fewer rated real players (none in 1980). A filler is not a 1980 player of that team: a
  player of the franchise's own slot in the 1980 save who is on no PFR 1980 roster (no name match
  with any real player, so no one is duplicated). Every filler is flagged "FILLER" in its flags file.
  Identity across saves: name (exact, nickname, initial, one/two-letter spelling), position
